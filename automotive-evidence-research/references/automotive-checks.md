# Automotive Engineering Checks

Before accepting a claim, check:

- vehicle model, model year, trim, market, prototype/production state, and hardware generation;
- component versus subsystem versus vehicle-level metric;
- peak, continuous, nominal, limit, target, simulation, bench, and vehicle-test values;
- speed, road friction, temperature, load, tire, SOC, voltage, current limit, and control mode;
- option availability versus standard fitment;
- patent applicant versus current owner, filing versus grant, and patent versus production implementation;
- supplier announcement versus OEM confirmation and actual SOP evidence;
- organizational identity across business units, subsidiaries, joint ventures, brands, and renamed companies;
- sample, concept, demonstration, nomination, development contract, SOP, and serial production;
- claimed control effect versus sensor availability, actuator authority, latency, and safety fallback.

For vehicle-dynamics results, preserve maneuver definition, driver/robot input, measurement convention, filtering, sign convention, and baseline configuration.
