# Image Provenance and Citation

Create one record per report image:

- figure ID and intended evidence role;
- image type: photo, render, patent, diagram, chart, crop, annotation, adaptation, or redraw;
- product, version, and date represented;
- original publisher and page/document title;
- canonical page URL and, when useful, direct asset URL;
- publication date, access date, page number, and original figure number;
- reuse or license status: permitted, restricted, unclear, or requires review;
- edits: none, crop, highlight, annotation, translation, compositing, or redraw;
- claim(s) supported and evidence state.

## Caption patterns

Original/cropped image:

`Figure N. Description. Source: Publisher, Document title, date, p./Fig.; cropped and annotated if applicable.`

Redraw:

`Figure N. Description. Redrawn from Source A [x] and Source B [y]; inferred elements are marked.`

Generated concept:

`Figure N. Conceptual illustration generated for explanation; not product evidence.`

Keep watermarks and copyright notices. Do not imply that public availability grants unrestricted reuse. For external publication, flag unclear rights for review and prefer official press assets, licensed images, links, or original explanatory redraws.
