# Source Routing

## Route by question

| Question | Start with | Then use |
|---|---|---|
| Product specification | official manual/product page | certification, OEM material, independent test |
| Production status | OEM/supplier filing or official launch | homologation, vehicle listing, plant or customer evidence |
| Mechanism | technical manual, patent, paper | teardown and multi-angle photographs |
| Performance | test report with method | independent test or comparable dataset |
| Supplier/customer relation | both companies' official material | filings, vehicle documentation, reputable trade press |
| Market or financial | filing, regulator, trade data | analyst report with disclosed method |
| Safety/regulation | regulator and standard | official interpretation and certification evidence |
| Patent landscape | patent family and legal status | citations, assignees, product evidence |

## Search expansion

Generate exact names, former names, abbreviations, translated engineering terms, site-specific queries, filetype queries, patent classifications, and known supplier/customer combinations. Search Chinese, English, and German when relevant.

## Retrieval discipline

Open the primary document rather than relying on snippets. Search within long documents for model names, units, figure references, revision history, and qualification language. Record negative results only as search gaps, not proof of nonexistence.
