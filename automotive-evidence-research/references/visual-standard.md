# Technical Visual Standard

## Default style

- Use a technical, restrained style with no decorative icons.
- Use a white or very light background, black text, and no more than three principal colors.
- Default to 16:9 when intended for slides; preserve report-specific aspect ratios otherwise.
- Use consistent fonts, stroke widths, arrows, corner radii, terminology, and alignment grid.
- Express hierarchy primarily through position, size, weight, and spacing rather than extra colors.
- Use Chinese primary labels with smaller English terms only when they add value.

## Legibility gate

Render at final use size. Reject overlap, clipping, malformed shapes, stretched photographs, ambiguous crossings, excessive text, weak contrast, inconsistent alignment, or labels too small to read.

## Composition gate

Keep the main engineering relationship visually dominant. Group related elements, maintain whitespace, minimize crossing connectors, align repeated elements, and use a clear reading order. Avoid low-density decoration and crowded one-page encyclopedias.

## Fidelity gate

Preserve realistic proportion and packaging in product visuals. Use vector or code-native drawing for exact diagrams. Label photorealistic generated imagery as conceptual; never use it as evidence of hidden product structure.

Release only after both the engineering gate and visual gate pass.
